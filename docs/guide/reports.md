# Site reports

A report is for anything wrong with a building that isn't part of a checklist - a broken door
closer, fly-tipping, graffiti, a security concern. Any signed-in role can raise one.

## Where to raise one

- **Staff / field agent:** the report button on a block's detail screen - the report is about
  that block.
- **Cleaner, on site:** the report button on the on-site card - the report is about that site
  and linked to your attendance.
- **Cleaner, any time:** the report button on the home - you pick the site, including fully
  offline from the cached list.

## Composing

- **Category:** Repair (the default), Cleaning, Waste, Safety, Security, Grounds, Antisocial,
  or Other.
- **Note:** required. A photo on its own is a puzzle for whoever reads it - say what and where.
  Up to 4000 characters.
- **Photos:** optional, up to 10. Same treatment as wizard photos - shrunk for upload,
  rotation fixed, camera or library.
- **Location:** the report tries to attach where you were, briefly and only if permitted.
  It never blocks or delays the send - no fix simply means no position on the report.

## Sending

Tap Send and the report is accepted **immediately** - it is saved on the phone and you can
walk away. With signal it goes at once; without, it queues. The report entry buttons show a
badge while anything is waiting: "2 reports waiting to send - they go when you have signal."

**Your reports** shows two lists: first anything still on this phone (answering "did it go?"
at a glance, including how many photos are still to upload), then everything already sent,
newest first.

## If a report can't be filed

If the server refuses a report (typically an assignment or account problem), its row in Your
reports gets a **Not sent** tag with the server's reason and two buttons:

- **Try again** - after the underlying problem is fixed. Sends the same report exactly once.
- **Discard** - asks first, naming the site and warning that the report and its photos will be
  permanently removed from this phone. Nothing on the server is touched.

Only a failed report can be discarded. One that is still waiting belongs to the queue and will
send itself; one that already sent is out of the phone's hands. While any failed report
exists, the entry-point hint says "needs attention in Your reports" instead of the waiting
message.
