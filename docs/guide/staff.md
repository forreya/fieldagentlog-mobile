# Staff

Sign in and you land on **Your blocks**: every block in your organisation. Everything in
[field-agent.md](field-agent.md) applies to you too - find, nearest, block detail, history,
starting checklists, reports. Two things are staff-only.

## Starting a checklist on any block

Staff don't need to be assigned. **Start checklist** on any of your organisation's blocks
creates the visit on the spot (recorded against your name and email) and opens the wizard.
On a block you don't have rights to, the app says so plainly.

## Plan visits

The **Plan visits** button builds rounds of nearby due blocks in driving order, worst first -
a suggestion for how to spend the day, not a commitment. Every row opens its block. Nothing is
saved: leave the screen and return and it plans afresh from the current state of the portfolio.
Field agents don't get this tool - it needs the whole-organisation view.
